Toy Town · your turnaround sheets · painted views, no 3D models

The Marines turn, walk and talk, in your painted style

Every view on screen is cut from the sheets you made: front, three-quarter, side and back, and five faces each for talking and blinking. When a Marine turns, the next painted view blends in with a slight squash, the way hand-painted games turn their characters.

The voice uses your browser's built-in speech, a placeholder until we choose real voices. Captions always show.

How it works

  • Six directions from four drawings: front, three-quarter, side and back, plus the three-quarter and side views mirrored for the other way round.
  • Talking and blinking: your five faces (smile, open, “oh”, teeth, eyes closed) are fitted onto the front body. The mouth follows the letters of each line.
  • Walking: for now a stiff toy march: a bob and a rock from foot to foot. The legs don't swing yet. That needs walk frames from you, see below.
  • Not 3D: nothing is modelled. The brushwork you see is your painting at every angle.
