Toy Marines in 3D

The same scene as the 2D test, now with real depth. One Marine walks in, takes the present in both hands and turns to face you, then carries it away. The other turns to you and waves. Drag across the scene to look around them.

What to judge

  • Built as toys: each Marine is made in code from simple solid shapes: a ball head, cylinder legs and body, a flared coat skirt, a wide white cap with a black peak, rounded gloves. They are rigged with real shoulders, elbows, hips and a neck, so the hands actually grip the present.
  • Painted, not plastic: no smooth 3D shading. Light falls in three flat painted bands from the upper left, every surface carries a stipple texture made with the map's palette, a soft dark outline goes round each part, and a paper grain lies over the whole scene.
  • Faces and uniforms are painted onto the shapes: eyes with an iris and a catchlight, brows, rosy cheeks, a mouth that opens with the words, gold buttons, a white belt with a buckle, the red stripe.
  • The honest question: does this sit in the painting, or does it look like a video game in front of it?
