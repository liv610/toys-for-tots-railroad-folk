Toy Town · all four of your sheets · painted frames only, nothing modelled

The Marines walk in, talk face to face, salute and wave

Every frame is cut from the sheets you made: the side walk cycle, the four turning views, the talking faces for the front and three-quarter views, the head turns, and the wave and salute poses.

The voice uses your browser's built-in speech, a placeholder until we choose real voices. Captions always show.

What's new

  • Real walking: your side walk cycle, the steps timed to the distance covered so the feet don't slide.
  • Talking face to face: your three-quarter mouth shapes, fitted onto the three-quarter body.
  • Glances: your head turns let them look at each other while standing front-on.
  • Salute and wave: your poses. The waving hand rocks at the elbow.
  • No white gaps: your new sheets came with transparent backgrounds, so the space between arms and legs is truly empty.
